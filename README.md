# Is a hot dog a sandwich? 

Provenance: Our data was collected from the dates 8/29/25 to 9/1/25 via a Google form distributed to undergraduate students (estimated ages 18-22) at the University of Virginia. 
Management Plan: Our data will be stored on this public repository, and may be freely used for educational purposes only.
Data Dictionary: 
  - year: the self-reported year of undergraduate college education being pursued by the respondent (1 = first-year/freshman, 2 = second-year/sophomore
